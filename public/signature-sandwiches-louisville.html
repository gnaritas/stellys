How they're built

A deli board with a chef's habits

The signature sandwiches run $14–16 and cover the deli classics plus whatever the kitchen is excited about this week. The bread comes from local bakers; the fillings are made in-house, down to the pastrami.

Specials ride alongside the regulars. A recent build — the kind of thing that shows up on this board: a collard green, pimento cheese, and apple butter hoagie with local collards and Amish apple butter made here.

  • Bread baked locally, fillings made in-house
  • Specials rotate — "very fun and odd takes" are on purpose
