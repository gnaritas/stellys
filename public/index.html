From Germantown to Shelby Park

The deli moved. The pastrami came with it.

Stelly's started as Goss Ave. Deli over in Germantown — a New York-style deli that got Louisville's attention after Chef Eric Morris's pastrami post went viral in early 2026. The Goss Avenue run closed in June, and the crew came back on July 24, 2026 with a new name and a stall inside Logan Street Market.

Same kitchen, same hands, new board. The pastrami is still house-made, the sandwiches are still stacked, and the specials still chase odd ideas.

"The balance of smoke, the right amount of steam, the ratio of good brown mustard to meat."

Eric Morris, on his pastrami rabbit hole — Courier Journal, Feb. 2026
