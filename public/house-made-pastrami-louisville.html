Why it's different

Smoke. Steam. Mustard. In balance.

Chef Eric Morris has been chasing pastrami since 2020 — what he calls his "pastrami rabbit hole". Not a recipe to follow, but a balance to hit: the smoke, the steam, the ratio of good brown mustard to meat.

It's cooked slow and steamed in-house, then sliced to order when you call it. When the pastrami is on, it's the board's headliner — and when it's not, the kitchen is already working on the next odd take.

  • Slow-cooked and steamed in-house, sliced to order
  • Served as the deli's signature — a pastrami rabbit hole, finally out of the hole
  • Listed on the Toast menu every day it's available
  • Pickup via order ahead, inside Logan Street Market

"I told myself I needed to perfect this and couldn't move past it. The balance of smoke, the right amount of steam, the ratio of good brown mustard to meat."

Eric Morris — Courier Journal, Feb. 2026
