Plain-words privacy

What this site knows about you

Short answer: almost nothing. This site sets no cookies, loads no advertising or tracking scripts, and collects no analytics. There is no account to make and nothing running in the background.

The contact form

If you send a note through the contact form, we ask for your name and message, and optionally a phone number or email. That information goes to the deli team and is used for one thing: answering you. It is not sold, not shared, and not used for marketing lists.

Ordering

Online ordering happens on Toast's own platform — once you tap Order online, their terms and privacy policy apply to the order itself.

Questions about privacy?

Send them through the contact form — same as any other note, straight to the deli.
